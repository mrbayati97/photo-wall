Questions

About cost, privacy, files and the people already on the wall.

Does Photo Wall cost anything?

No. Browsing, posting, likes and follows are free.

Who can see the photos I post?

Anyone. Every photo gets a public link.

Can I change or delete a photo?

Yes. Open your pin to edit it, or delete it with its photo.

Does Photo Wall store my email?

Only a hash of it, never the address itself.

Why are my saved photos missing on my phone?

Saved photos stay in the browser you saved them in.
